=== How to read this ===

- Exact McNemar asks only about pairs that disagree, so a practice both arms
  always follow (or never follow) contributes nothing either way.
- q values control the false discovery rate within each family. A raw p
  below 0.05 with q above 0.10 is a lead, not a result.
- These measures are not in the study's pre-registered metric list; they are a
  second question asked of the same repos, and should be described that way.
